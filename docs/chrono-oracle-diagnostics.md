## Observations that qualified the oracle

### Legacy target replay

A fresh Chrono 10.0.0 replay matched the stored `A0_cal_full10mm` maps within
`0.00076 mm`. The old target was therefore not corrupt or generated by a stale
binary. It was retired because its free-centered load and residual-time
contract were not qualified.

### Translation and phase test

After applying `valid_heightmap_mask.npy`, the deformation centroid moved with
the translated cylinder. The earlier grid-lock conclusion came from including
the invalid boundary ring and is withdrawn.

The 10 mm test still showed millimetre-scale phase sensitivity:

| Episode | Commanded center (mm) | Valid-interior centroid (mm) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| centered | `(0, 0)` | `(0.426, 0.000)` |
| x half-cell | `(5, 0)` | `(7.723, 0.000)` |
| x/y half-cell | `(5, 5)` | `(6.829, 5.000)` |

This justified a guided repeat and finer oracle resolution; it did not justify
a classifier or an additional Genesis parameter.

### Guided loading

Adding the vertical guide removed lateral drift and spin without changing SCM
soil/contact physics. The `(0, +5 mm)` offset was selected for repeatable,
cleaner cross-sections, not because the offset is physically privileged.

The accepted 10 mm episode established the timing protocol. The corresponding
5 mm repeat then became the active oracle and superseded the 10 mm R&D target.

### Initial-surface checks

The active oracle's initial H0 is flat and internally consistent. A
20 mm-particle Genesis control and the accepted 5 mm-particle/n128 preparation
both reconstruct it successfully. The promoted n128 bed reaches H0 RMSE
`0.070 mm` and maximum error `0.237 mm`. The prior large n128 errors were
caused by an under-sampled Genesis particle/grid discretization, not Chrono H0.

## Actions taken after oracle qualification

1. Retired all `A0_cal_full10mm` observations from calibration.
2. Fixed the Chrono action with a vertical guide and a declared y offset.
3. Recorded the speed-and-hold loading gate and exact residual duration.
4. Repeated the protocol at 5 mm.
5. Applied the valid-mask boundary exclusion to all comparisons.
6. Kept the Chrono episode fixed while Genesis resolution and parameters were
   tested.
7. Promoted Genesis to a ratio-matched 5 mm-particle/n128 bed without changing
   Chrono or using a geostatic stress multiplier.

## Current downstream Genesis result

Subsequent frozen-contract studies selected
`E=20.432828 kPa`, `phi=14.727053 deg`, and `nu=0.101894536`. Exact replay
`r2at0vvb` confirmed objective `8.704 mm`, loaded RMSE `1.864 mm`,
residual-footprint RMSE `13.678 mm`, and residual signed mean `+12.941 mm`.
This improvement changes neither the accepted oracle nor its qualification.

Retained-raw replay `ykep3esa` generated aligned isometric point-cloud and
signed 2D DEM-error views plus surface/raw-particle PCDs. Its aggregate result
was stable, but it remains visualization evidence only because four residual
projection cells exceeded the frozen three-cell sparse-bin allowance.
