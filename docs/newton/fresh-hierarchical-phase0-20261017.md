## Why fresh execution is required

Inspection of the active runners found no explicit random sampling in the
Chrono cylinder path. That makes deterministic behavior plausible, but it does
not establish it. The old Chrono manifests omit source revision, relevant
source hashes, thread policy, and a same-build variation comparison. Newton records
Python, Newton, Warp, and device versions, but the old campaign likewise lacks
a same-build duplicate gate and complete input/source hashes.

Determinism cannot be guaranteed from absence of a random-number call. Fresh
repeats therefore measure variation for the pinned build, device, inputs,
command, and execution policy. Bitwise identity is useful diagnostic evidence,
but it is not a prerequisite for starting the campaign. Old outputs remain
quarantined because they lack this provenance and were generated by the prior
routine, not because every future output must be exactly identical.

## Stage 0: same-build numerical-variation characterization

At the start of the campaign:

1. run the centered 31 N Chrono action twice, sequentially, in independent
   directories;
2. require both actions to be accepted, then report acceptance-time and
   initial/loaded/residual height-map differences;
3. generate the metric particle bed independently from each fresh H0 and
   report whether PLY and metadata digests match;
4. run the centered 31 N Newton response twice, sequentially, on the same
   device, with independent fresh bed construction and preparation;
5. report max, mean, and RMS differences for surface maps and persisted
   particle-state arrays, plus exact-value identity as a diagnostic.

The duplicate runs are qualification evidence and are not added twice to the
training set. Nonzero differences become a measured uncertainty floor. They do
not stop matrix expansion by themselves. Stop only if a repeat is incomplete,
unaccepted, non-finite, produced under a different software/device environment,
or has incompatible shapes/support. Do not average repeats to hide variation.

This test characterizes same-build, same-device variation. It does not promise
bitwise determinism or portability to another GPU, package build, or source
tree. The residual improvement must ultimately be materially larger than the
measured variation; that comparison is evidence, not a preselected microscopic
tolerance.

## Hierarchical admission

### Level 0 — provenance and variation characterization

Hard gate for provenance, not bitwise equality. The run belongs to the fresh
campaign and has complete hashes and environment identity. Stage 0 variation
must be characterized before the final residual result is interpreted, but
matrix generation need not wait for exact equality.

### Level 1 — numerical integrity

Hard gate. Preparation converges, all states are finite, the guide constraint
passes, all 14,161 surface cells are available, and initial surface support is
at least 95%.

### Level 2 — interaction artifacts

Classification and uncertainty gate, not a strict zero-overlap gate.
Penetration depth, affected fraction, loaded-state fraction, persistence, and
contact behavior are reported. Only gross interaction failure blocks use:
penetration exceeding 10% of particle spacing or more than 1% of particles
inside the analytic indenter.

A usable run can therefore be labelled usable_with_contact_artifact. It cannot
be called strictly mechanics-qualified.

### Level 3 — geometric prior utility

Primary Phase 0 physics gate. Error is evaluated in the action footprint and
within twice the foot radius. Whole-bed RMSE is retained only as a background
sanity measure.

For loaded and residual states, the MPM response must have positive MSE skill
relative to predicting no deformation on both primary masks:

    skill = 1 - MSE(MPM, Chrono) / MSE(no deformation, Chrono)

Peak depression, mean footprint depression, displaced volume, deformation
footprint, and centroid are reported even when they are not hard gates.

### Level 4 — residual exit

The residual is evaluated on whole held-out positions. On every validation and
test episode, the corrected result must improve over both raw MPM and the
no-deformation baseline on the footprint and twice-radius masks. Training-cell
holdouts do not satisfy this gate.

### Level 5 — Gaussian and planner exit

Later gates measure corrected Gaussian geometry and fixed open-loop planner
ranking. They cannot be claimed from surface-map or residual training results.
